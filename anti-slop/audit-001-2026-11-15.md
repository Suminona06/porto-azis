## Findings Fixed During Implementation & Polish

1. **[Resolved | Motion Integrity | `src/components/portfolio/PreloaderCurtain.astro`]**
   - **Finding**: Early Chromium frame sampling showed the preloader progress tween (`0 -> 92`) and font-ready exit tween (`-> 100`) racing if `document.fonts.ready` resolved before `0.72s`, causing counter output to briefly hit `100%` before reverting to `92%`.
   - **Resolution**: Killed the initial progress tween (`tl.kill()`) inside `waitForFonts.then(...)` prior to running the completion timeline, ensuring strictly monotonic `0% -> 100%` progression.

2. **[Resolved | Copy & Mobile Flow | `src/components/portfolio/HeroSection.astro`]**
   - **Finding**: Initial hero footer markup rendered `{copy.basedIn}: {profile.location}`, producing duplicated `"BASED IN INDONESIA: INDONESIA"` / `"BERBASIS DI INDONESIA: INDONESIA"` copy and pushing the mobile hero footer onto two stacked rows on `320px`/`375px`.
   - **Resolution**: Simplified the interactive location button label to display `{locationLabel}` directly (with full accessible context preserved via `aria-label={`${copy.basedIn}: ${profile.location}`}`), keeping the location button and `hh:mm A (GMT +7)` clock aligned on a single horizontal row across `320px`, `375px`, `768px`, and `1280px` (`sameFooterRow: true`, `44px` tap targets).

3. **[Resolved | Keyboard Focus Affordance | `src/styles/global.css`]**
   - **Finding**: Elements styled with Tailwind v4 `transition-colors` inside inverse pills (`LocaleToggle` active option) initially transitioned `outline-color` from `currentColor` (`#FFFFFF` over white background) toward `--focus-ring` over `150ms`, creating a brief low-contrast ring frame upon `Tab` focus.
   - **Resolution**: Pinned `:where(a, button, summary, input, textarea, select, [tabindex]) { outline-color: var(--focus-ring); }` in `@layer base` so `:focus-visible` renders an immediate `2px solid` `#000000` (Light) / `#FFFFFF` (Dark) outline on all 10 sampled keyboard stops.

4. **[Resolved | Reduced Motion Side Effects | `src/styles/global.css` & `src/components/portfolio/PreloaderCurtain.astro`]**
   - **Finding**: Legacy global `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` rule enforced `0.01ms !important` universally across all elements; additionally, live OS switching of `prefers-reduced-motion` while the preloader ran needed cleanup.
   - **Resolution**: Scoped reduced-motion rules explicitly to motion targets (`html` smooth scroll, marquee tracks, `.hero-motion-mask`, `[data-hero-bio-word]`, `[data-reveal-word]`), preserved smooth non-spatial hover color transitions, and wired live `MediaQueryList` listeners in both `PreloaderCurtain.astro` and `landing-motion.ts` (`ctx.revert()`).

5. **[Resolved | Every-Load Preloader & Reload Jitter Polish | `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro`, `src/styles/global.css`, `src/components/portfolio/PreloaderCurtain.astro`, `src/components/portfolio/HeroSection.astro`, `src/lib/motion/text-scramble.ts`, `src/lib/motion/landing-motion.ts`]**
   - **Finding**: Reloading `/` skipped the preloader via `sessionStorage`, briefly exposed settled Hero content before deferred scripts faded it out, toggled scrollbar width (`1280px` -> `1265px`) during `preloader-active` removal, paused mid-curtain at `yPercent: 0`, and caused proportional Playfair Display cipher frames plus raw `requestAnimationFrame` loops to jitter during Hero decode. Additionally, reserving `scrollbar-gutter: stable` left a light vertical scrollbar strip next to the `#000000` preloader in Light mode.
   - **Resolution**: Removed `sessionStorage` skip gating so `/` runs the preloader on every load/reload (bypassed only when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`), forced `history.scrollRestoration = 'manual'` + instant `(0, 0)` reset, locked `html` scrollbar gutter with dark `#000000` scrollbar styling while `preloader-active` is mounted (`clientWidth` constant at `1265px`, `0px` horizontal jump), unified all cipher/scramble intervals onto `gsap.to` (`createCipherDecodeTween`) inside layout-locked Hero word sizers (`h1Height` constant at `282px`), pre-staged the Hero GSAP timeline behind the continuous `600ms` (`yPercent: 100 -> -100`, `force3D: true`) curtain pass, and locked location button `minWidth` (`76px`) during hover scramble.
